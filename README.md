## Projects

**frameSnatcher**
FrameSnatcher is an easy way to have your agent cut video. Use long form video and direct it to what you want to see. It scans through all the frames and only pulls out the ones you asked it to. That way you get high resolution frames you can actually use. A great collection of images for your projects, videos, images, anything.

**SizzleMaker**
SizzleMaker is a way to create incredible sizzle videos with your agent. All you need to do is give it a public Drive link with your video files and it will extract them and use them. It can put overlays on top. It can cut to music and beats if you give it your own MP3 or WAV file. You can also add voice overs. Its a great way to craft a sizzle video. Ideal to use with Muse, but it also works with Claude or ChatGPT agents.

**SuperStylin**
SuperStylin helps you put together new clothing combinations with your own clothing. It takes new trends from Instagram and fashion trends and applies them to what you already have, for any occasion. Just upload clean photos of your items laid out on the floor or bed, each one in full view. Not pictures of an open closet. The AI cleans them up, indexes them and categorizes them for you. Add new clothes as you go.

**NFT Content Backup**
NFT Content Backup lets you back up your NFT metadata across any chain. You just provide the wallet address of the wallet you want to back up. You do not have to connect your wallet directly. It backs up the IPFS files too, the images and the video, so you have an archive even if the blockchain dies.
